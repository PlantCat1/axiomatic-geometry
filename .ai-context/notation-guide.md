# Notation Guide for Maloney's Geometry

## Points & Sets
- $\mathbf{P}$ = the plane (set of all points)
- $A, B, C$ = individual points (capital italic)

## Relations & Operations
- $\mathcal{B}(A,B,C)$ = betweenness; "$B$ between $A$ and $C$"
- $AB \cong CD$ = congruence of segments
- $S(A,B,\ell)$ = same-side relation; "$A, B$ on same side of line $\ell$"

## Geometric Objects
- $AB$ = segment (set of $A$, $B$, and all points between)
- $\overrightarrow{AB}$ = ray from $A$ through $B$
- $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ = line through $A$ and $B$
- $\triangle ABC$ = triangle (union of three segments)
- $\angle ABC$ = angle (union of rays $\overrightarrow{BA}$ and $\overrightarrow{BC}$)
- $\text{Int}(\angle ABC)$ = interior of angle $\angle ABC$
- $H_{\ell,A}$ = half-plane determined by line $\ell$ and point $A$

## Common Abbreviations in Proofs
- "By Axiom N" = reference numbered axiom
- "By Theorem N" = reference numbered theorem
- "By definition" = use formal definition from text
